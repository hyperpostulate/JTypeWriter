A quiet place to write.

Open it and the screen holds nothing but your text: one page, one font, the colour you picked. Everything else stays out of the way until you ask for it.

Download JTypeWriter

Free. Linux and Windows. No account, no e-mail address, no trial period.

Why JTypeWriter?

  • Your words stay in your file

    The document you write is the plain text file you chose, on your own computer. Nothing is uploaded, nothing is synchronised, and nothing waits for a connection.

  • A page you cannot lose by accident

    Every save keeps the previous version beside your file, and what you are still working on is set aside every half minute. If the power goes out, it is offered back the next time you open the editor.

  • Only you and the page

    No account, no sign-in, no cloud, no advertising, no software nagging you to install something. The window holds your text and one calm line of numbers at the bottom.

  • A keyboard that answers back

    A typewriter click as you type, at the volume you choose. Three themes, six text colours, and a word count that keeps score while you go.
